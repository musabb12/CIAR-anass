Android Play Store image mapping

Place these image assets under `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/`:

- featureGraphic.png
  * Google Play feature graphic
  * Recommended size: 1024x500

- graphic.png
  * Play Store promotional graphic / icon background
  * Recommended size: 1024x500

- phoneScreenshots/0.png
- phoneScreenshots/1.png
- phoneScreenshots/2.png
  * Phone screenshot placeholders
  * Recommended size: 1080x1920 or 1242x2208

Additional asset notes:
- Use landscape or portrait screenshots that demonstrate app features.
- Replace placeholder PNGs with production artwork before upload.
- For localized variants, duplicate the `en-US` folder under `metadata/android/` with the locale code.
