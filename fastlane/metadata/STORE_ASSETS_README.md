Store asset placeholders

This folder contains placeholder image assets for both Google Play and App Store metadata.

Android:
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/featureGraphic.png` (recommended 1024x500)
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/graphic.png` (recommended 1024x500)
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/0.png`
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/1.png`
- `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/2.png`

iOS:
- `fastlane/metadata/ios/en-US/screenshots/iPhone-1.png`
- `fastlane/metadata/ios/en-US/screenshots/iPhone-2.png`
- `fastlane/metadata/ios/en-US/screenshots/iPad-1.png`

Recommended sizes:
- Google Play feature graphic: 1024x500
- Google Play icon: 512x512
- Google Play phone screenshots: 1080x1920 or 1242x2208
- App Store iPhone screenshots: 1242x2688 / 1125x2436
- App Store iPad screenshots: 2048x2732

Replace each placeholder with production artwork before final upload.
